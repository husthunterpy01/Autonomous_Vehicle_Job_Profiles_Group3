## 1. Purpose of the Meeting

This was the first check-in between Group 3 and the capstone facilitator, Sumayyah Ahmad. The facilitator outlined expectations for client meetings, group operating guidelines, project management practices, and how the group will be assessed over the course of the project.

## 2. Client Meeting Attendance

- At least two group members must attend every client meeting so that if one person misses something, the other can record it. The full group does not need to attend.
- Discuss all outstanding questions internally before meeting the client.
- Send the client an agenda in advance so they know what will be discussed.

## 3. Operating Guidelines to Develop

- **Timeline expectations** — to be documented in the operating guideline and communicated clearly to the client so there are no surprises.
- **Contingency planning** — a clear process for what happens if a group member falls sick or is unavailable, including who takes over their work.
- **Project manager / group lead elections** — the process for electing leads, noting at least three group leads are required on a rotating basis over the course of the project.
- **Logbook contributions and shared group accountability** — how the group will hold itself and individual members accountable, with clear communication to avoid later issues.
- **Project management methodology** — the group has decided, following discussion with the client, to follow an Azure DevOps/Scrum approach. The operating guideline should record the reasons for choosing this methodology along with its pros and cons.
- **Communication record-keeping** — how team communications are logged, including any communication delays with the client or within the group.
- **Risk management** — risks identified during the project and the plan to manage them.
- **Trust building** — a short section on how the group plans to build trust between members.
- **Handling changing client requirements** — how the group will decide whether to accommodate scope changes requested by the client versus flagging items as out of scope, and the reasoning behind each decision.

## 4. Assessment Criteria

The facilitator advised she will be grading the group on:

- New learnings gained over the course of the project.
- Roles performed by each member (e.g. minute taker), including what each person was supposed to do, what they accomplished, and where they are still lacking.
- Professionalism and collaboration during meetings — conversation should flow smoothly between members via an agreed agenda, rather than members asking "who's next."
- Project management — including whether the whole team has agreed on the chosen methodology.
- Decision-making and risk management.
- Individual contribution — every member must speak during meetings to receive a mark; the facilitator will observe from next week onward with camera and microphone off.
- Progress against the project timeline — some groups use a Gantt chart or screen-share of front-end/back-end work to demonstrate progress.
- Reflections on what was learned over the course of the project.

## 5. Meeting Format Going Forward

- From next week, the facilitator will join, turn off her camera and microphone, and observe the group's collaboration and decision-making rather than lead the discussion.
- Meetings run for approximately half an hour; time should be managed so every member gets the opportunity to speak.
- The group should send the agenda and meeting link to the facilitator in advance of each session.

## 6. Action Items

- Decide and document the client meeting attendance roster (minimum two attendees per meeting).
- Draft the operating guideline covering: timelines, contingency planning, group lead rotation (minimum 3 leads), logbook contributions, accountability, methodology (Azure Scrum) with pros/cons, communication logging, risk management, trust building, and change-request handling.
- Prepare and circulate an agenda ahead of each client and facilitator meeting.
- Confirm the schedule of group lead rotation (at least three leads across the project).
- Continue producing meeting minutes that capture each member's planned task, completed work, and outstanding work.
- Send today's meeting notes/transcription to the group by email.
