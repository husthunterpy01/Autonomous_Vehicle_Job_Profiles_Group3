2.2 First Client Discussion and Domain Guidance

During the first client discussion, the team clarified the intended direction of the Autonomous Vehicle Job Profiles project. The project was discussed as a specialised platform that would collect and organise autonomous-vehicle-related job information from multiple companies rather than requiring users to visit individual career websites.

Key discussion points included:

Clarifying what the client expected the project to achieve.

Discussing how the proposed job-profile website should present jobs and related information.

Identifying technical skills, tools, technologies, qualifications, and wider role requirements from advertisements.

Using public company career pages as the main source of job advertisements.

Keeping links to original job advertisements for traceability.

Supporting both curriculum planning and end-user job-market exploration.

Following the meeting, Adrian sent the team additional resources, including AI job-market references, Waymo salary information, Autoware architecture documentation, the Waymo Open Dataset, nuScenes, and an initial list of approximately 42 autonomous-vehicle companies.

The company list provided a concrete starting point for the project's data-collection scope and later informed the team's investigation of different recruitment platforms and API-based scraping approaches.

6. Current MVP Direction

Based on the client discussions and project work completed so far, the proposed MVP is a read-only autonomous-vehicle job exploration platform.

The current direction includes:

Collect publicly available job advertisements from relevant AV companies.

Provide a central interface for searching and comparing job opportunities.

Extract and structure skills, technologies, tools, qualifications, functional areas, and role profiles.

Include adjacent technical roles where their responsibilities are relevant to AV systems, even when the job title itself does not explicitly mention autonomous vehicles.

Preserve original source links and collection information for traceability.

Provide dashboard or trend views, initially using mock data where live integration is not yet complete.
