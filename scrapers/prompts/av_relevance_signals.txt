AV RELEVANCE SIGNALS
Use these to decide whether a posting is AV-relevant (is_av_relevant). General
automotive, corporate, or unrelated software roles are NOT AV relevant even if
the employer builds AV products elsewhere. Conventional vehicle engineering is
general automotive: powertrain/engine/transmission and EV propulsion or battery
work (including powertrain calibration on dynos and proving grounds), brakes and
chassis, crash/passive safety, NVH, seating, thermal, and humanoid or other
non-vehicle robotics.

Cross-cutting signals (strong, not category-specific):
Autoware, ApolloAuto, Apollo Cyber, Cyber RT, ROS 2, rclcpp, autonomous driving, autonomous vehicle, self-driving, autonomy, ADAS, LiDAR, radar, sensor fusion, perception, motion planning, path planning, localization, robotics, computer vision

Dataset/benchmark names (secondary signal on their own):
Waymo Open Dataset, nuScenes, Argoverse, Argoverse 2, KITTI, BDD100K
